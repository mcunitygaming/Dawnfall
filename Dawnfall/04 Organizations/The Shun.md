The Shun acts as a secret government system of people who infiltrate kingdoms and settlements to gather information on all forms of corruption. It was created by one of the three main characters, Corvelan Aldren McCaleth, after his 4-year term at Sunbreak Spire, and does not act within normal governments without permission from the respected kingdom. It will instead give all information on evil to the leaders of the kingdom. Still, if the highest authority is corrupt, then the organization may act directly. The members of the Shun are ranked by the regions they are responsible for, which serve as their titles, along with Aliases, for example, Mr. World-Class Rook. This means that even some members of Shun don't know each other's true identities. The organization is feared by the evil, loved by the good, and debated by all whether it even exists. A requirement for being in the Kingdom and World-class spots is having one or both of a God Spirit and a Prime Vessel Gene.

### The Titles

- **World Class** | Leader of the Shun
- **Kingdom Class** | One person per kingdom
- **Region Class** | 2-4 people per Kingdom
- **County Class** | 2-4 per Region
- **City Class** | 2-4 per County
- **Precinct Class** | 2-4 per City

### Rules of Engagement

- True identities are **highly restricted**. Only the World Class and perhaps Kingdom Class members may know certain subordinates’ real names.
- Members operate independently unless a **high-level crisis** requires collaboration.
- **Ethics & Code:** The Shun operates with minimal collateral damage; any abuse of power is purged swiftly and secretly.
