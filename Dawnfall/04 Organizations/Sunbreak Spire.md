# Base Information

Sunbreak Spire is a school for all races of people, located in one of the pocket [[Realms]] which is owned by the headmaster. The students attend the school for 4 years; their 1st year starts with a **letter** being sent to their door, and after opening and reading it, a portal forms that leads to the entrance hall. After the entrance ceremony, which is just a speech by the Headmaster and the Student Council President, the 1st years will be put through **acceptance tests** to determine whether they stay at Sunbreak. Once they have passed the tests, the students will get a ring that gives a multitude of perks. During the Acceptance Tests, every applicant is evaluated not only for admission eligibility but also for their **mental structure, cognitive potential, and learning compatibility**. These results are recorded in a standardized profile used by faculty, the Student Council, and the Disciplinary Committee for placement and training optimization.

## 1. Mental Ability Measurement

Each student is assessed in three core cognitive fields:

### Core Cognitive Stats

All stats are measured on a standardized scale where:
**100 = Average Sunbreak entrant baseline**

**Stats:**

- **Spatial Awareness** – Perception of environment, positioning, motion tracking, battlefield awareness
- **Memory** – Retention, recall speed, pattern storage, and magical theory absorption
- **Processing Speed** – Decision-making speed, mental reaction time, and cognitive adaptation under pressure

### Format Example:

**Spatial Awareness / Memory / Processing Speed**  
Example: **120 / 95 / 110**

#### Two-State Evaluation System
Each stat is recorded in two separate values:

##### 1. Current Ability
The applicant’s present cognitive performance under controlled testing conditions.

##### 2. Maximum Potential
The highest estimated stable development ceiling achievable through:

- training
- environmental pressure
- magical maturation
- experience exposure

##### Format Example:
Current: **110 / 100 / 105**  
Max: **180 / 140 / 200**

## 2. Learning Method Distribution System

Each student is assigned a **100% cognitive distribution profile**, representing how they naturally absorb and process information.

This is split across three learning categories:

### Learning Types

#### Verbal Learning (Auditory)

- Learns through speech, explanation, instruction, and dialogue
- Strong in theory, coordination, and leadership communication

#### Visual Learning (Observation)

- Learns through seeing patterns, demonstrations, and spatial modeling
- Strong in magic replication, combat reading, and analysis

#### Tactical Learning (Action-Based)

- Learns through doing, repetition, physical experience, and trial-and-error
- Strong in combat adaptation, physical magic use, and instinct development

#### Format Rule

All three must total **100% exactly**:

Example Profile:

- Verbal: **35%**
- Visual: **30%**
- Tactical: **35%**

- Campus-wide teleportation (non-restricted zones)

#### 2. Event & Mission Control

### Student Council - SC

#### Structure

- **President (1)** – responsibility, not power
- **Council Members (4)** – equal authority

#### President Role

- Breaks 2–2 deadlocks
- Public representative
- Direct staff contact
- Cannot override unanimous decisions

#### Council Members

- Equal voting power
- Independent motion proposals
- One veto per term
- Assigned focus (not rank):
    - Administration (Student Judgment/ Rule Book)
    - Academics (Student Grades / Teaching Curriculum)
    - Student Affairs (Student Events / School Utilities)
    - External Affairs (Student Missions / Selling + Buying)

#### Powers

- Approve clubs
- Propose rule changes
- Recommend punishments
- Coordinate events
- Assign student-led missions
- At any time, Student Council members may request assistance from the Disciplinary Committee for any and all needs. Disciplinary Committee members are expected to respond immediately when called, prioritizing Student Council requests unless overridden by higher authority or critical threat level situations.

#### Limitations

- No enforcement
- No restricted zone access
- Must defer in danger scenarios

#### Reputation
Prestigious, influential, future leaders.

### Disciplinary Committee - DC

#### Structure

- **President (1)** – coordination authority
- **Committee Members (4)** – equal enforcement authority

#### President Role

- Activates emergency protocols
- Faculty liaison
- Limited override only during active threats

#### Committee Members

- Temporary detainment authority
- Temporary Archmage’s Tower access
- Threat response initiation
- Assigned patrol zones (not rank)
    - Learning District (Classrooms)
    - Combat District (Training Arenas)
    - Residential District (Dorms)
    - Commercial District (Markets)

#### Powers

- Rule enforcement
- Suppression tools
- Emergency overrides

#### Limitations

- Faculty review required
- Excessive force investigated
- God Spirit users are treated with caution.

#### Reputation
Feared, respected, whispered about.

## Uniform System

### Standard Academic Uniform

**Primary Use: Classrooms**  
**Who Wears It: Normal Students**

#### Components

- Upper garment: a cloak that reaches a little below the knees
- Inner layer: long or short-sleeved shirt
- Lower garment: choice between pants, shorts, or a skirt
- Footwear: Boots or dress shoes
- Optional pieces: Upper garments

#### Visual Markers

- School insignia: a mountain with a different amount of detail and structure on it
    - Year identification:
        - 1st = a mountain
        - 2nd = a mountain with a tent
        - 3rd = a mountain with a house
        - 4th = a mountain with a city

#### Variants

- Racial accommodations: allow for customization on any extra limbs or cultural differences
- Lab-compatible version: disposable white coat cover-up for labs
- Uniform color: an optional choice of color of the uniform, but the colors have to be neutral or a darker shade.
- Long cloak that reaches below the knee
- Long sleeves cover up for extra warmth.
- Light and heavy boots or dress shoes of choice

#### Restrictions

- Prohibited modifications: no getting rid of defense protections / keep appropriate for school environments
- Prohibited enchantments: strength boosters and/or curse charms
- Enforcement authority: the faculty and student council make and enforce rules (no physical enforcement)
