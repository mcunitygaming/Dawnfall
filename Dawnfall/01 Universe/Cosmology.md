In **Aurvara**, the supreme creator is the **Christian God** — the singular origin of all existence. From Him came **Ten Governing Angels**, each embodying a fundamental law of reality. These Angels are not “gods” by creation, but function as divine administrators of the world and are worshiped as such.

They do not typically interfere directly. Instead, their influence manifests through:
- The [[Magic Arts]] system
	- Laws
		- Physical
		- Elemental
		- Irregular
		- Mythical
	- Sacrifice
		- Beings can offer items to progress further in strength (This will lead to the sacrifice of life with a soul causing corruption by Thoughtborn)

The Ten Angels operate in balance:
- Physical enables motion.
- Elemental sustains the environment.
- Irregular governs the structure of reality.
- Light and Dark counterweight presence and absence.

No Angel rules above another — except in origin.

1. Above them all remains the Christian God
2. God created the Creation Angel
3. Next came the Space, Time, and Matter Angels
4. Later created was the Physical Angel
5. Then the Elemental Angels were made
6. Lastly came the Light and Dark Angels

## Physical Angel
**Domain:** Motion, force, stamina, and the structure of the body

- Governs Physical Magic.
- Oversees strength, endurance, reflex, and kinetic force.
- Ensures matter obeys impact and momentum.
- Most universally influential Angel in daily life.
- Bestows God spirits onto people with the help of the other angels

All living things unknowingly draw from this Angel.

## Elemental Angels
These Angels govern the four primal elemental forces. They represent tangible, visible power within the natural world. They often work together and mix to create and govern sub-elements. It is known that they bless the [[World Trees]] to bring magic into this world.

- **Fire Angel**
This Angel governs flame, heat, combustion, and energetic transformation. Associated with intensity, passion, destruction, and renewal.

- **Water Angel**
This Angel governs water, flow, currents, and adaptability. Associated with healing, emotion, patience, and persistence.

- **Earth Angel**
This Angel governs stone, soil, metal, and structure. Associated with endurance, growth, stability, and permanence.

- **Air Angel**
This Angel governs wind, breath, vibration, and sound. Associated with freedom, speed, and communication.

## Irregular Angels
These Angels govern deeper laws of existence. They are rarer, more abstract, and often misunderstood.

- **Space Angel**
This Angel is said to hold all the space that matter occupies together. Governs distance, position, compression, and expansion. Without it, matter would drift apart or collapse inward.

- **Time Angel**
This Angel is the one who pushes existence forward. Governs causality, aging, sequence, and progression. Time cannot be stopped — only redirected or strained. Although it does see all of time simultaneously, it exists with the time it's moving.

- **Matter Angel**
This Angel created the structure of reality and the other Angels. Governs Creation Magic and structural transmutation. The most mysterious Angel in doctrine. God created this angel first and then, through it, created the other angels, but this does not put it in a position of power, as it can only create, not destroy.

## Mythical Angels
These are angels that give freedom of choice, absence, and presence.

- **Light Angel**
This Angel embodies clarity, revelation, purification, and truth. Balances the Dark Angel. Governs Light Magic. Often associated with healing and anti-corruption effects.

- **Dark Angel**
This Angel, although not evil, governs darkness, shadow, entropy, depth, and unseen space. It doesn't interact with the normal world very often. Still, it's the only one that occupies physical space, as it lives in the parallel magic-particle world, ensuring people don't influence it too much. In rare cases where individuals accidentally enter or glimpse the Magic Realm, they may encounter its presence. These encounters are often overwhelming, leading to distorted interpretations, such as believing he was cast down and shunned by the other angels, causing him to be despised by people, but this is a wrong interpretation. Actually, God assigned him the task of protecting the world of magic particles.
