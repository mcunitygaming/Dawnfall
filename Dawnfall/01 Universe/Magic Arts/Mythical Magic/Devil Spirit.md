Dark magic can cause a loss of self-identity, making a **Devil Spirit** form. Devil Spirits are catastrophic manifestations formed when a being with a Dark Magic core loses their identity to **overwhelming negative emotion or external corruption**. Unlike God Spirits, Devil Spirits do not synchronize with their host—they consume them. Once fully formed, the host becomes a living source of Dreadmire, hostile to all life. Artificial Dark cores may be **purified** before full manifestation; innate Dark cores cannot. After corruption, you will appear as a tall, skinny zombie with gray skin and smoke emanating from your body.
