**Symbolizes the physical body perfected**

The **Vessel Form** is a rare genetic-magical trait that allows a person to awaken a permanent enhanced physical state known as the **Vessel Frame**. It represents the full physical potential of a living being, shaped by their body type, instincts, combat style, and Physical Magic compatibility. Unlike armor or external augmentation, the Vessel Form is the user’s own body **fully optimized and expanded through Physical Magic refinement**. It is a permanent-toggle state that can be activated or deactivated at will once awakened.

Vessel users do not gain a new body—they enter a **physically restructured version of themselves**, where muscle density, bone structure, reflex speed, and endurance are enhanced to match their deepest physical identity. This form reflects how the user’s body would evolve at its peak under perfect Physical Magic mastery.

## Appearance

- The Vessel Frame **physically amplifies the user’s natural body proportions**, typically increasing height by 25%.
- Body composition changes based on combat identity:
    - Agile fighters become **lean, tall, and highly muscular in a refined way.**
    - Power-based fighters become **heavily built, dense, and tank-like**
- Physical features subtly shift to reflect combat instincts (posture, stance, readiness, muscle distribution).
- The body often appears more “perfected” or “refined,” as if every movement system has been optimized.
- A faint **Physical Magic pressure aura** may radiate from the body, especially during activation.

## Function / Abilities

1. **Physical Amplification**
    1. The body undergoes full Physical Magic reinforcement, drastically increasing strength, speed, endurance, and durability.
    2. Muscle fibers and bone structure are reorganized for maximum efficiency and combat performance.

2. **Combat Identity Expression**
    1. The form adapts to the user’s natural fighting style, enhancing repeated instincts and movement patterns.
    2. It does not change personality but **exaggerates physical tendencies already present in combat behavior**.

3. **Sustained Activation State**
    1. Once unlocked, the Vessel Form becomes a **toggleable permanent state**, not a temporary transformation.
    2. Users can remain in this form for extended periods, but it consumes Physical Magic reserves continuously to keep the body stable, especially when the body is injured.

4. **Physical Magic Optimization**
    1. Improves internal flow and efficiency of Physical Magic usage.
    2. Enhances physical reinforcement techniques and close-combat synergy.

## Requirements for Awakening

- **Extreme Physical or Survival Pressure:** The body must be pushed beyond normal biological limits under lethal or near-lethal conditions.
- **Instinctual Combat Alignment:** The user must have a deeply ingrained physical combat style or survival instinct pattern that the body can “lock into.”
- **Magical Stability Threshold:** Sufficient Physical Magic capacity and control must be present to prevent bodily collapse during transformation.

## Effects on the Host

- Grants **massively enhanced physical performance**, allowing superhuman combat capability.
- Permanently alters baseline physical potential when unlocked.
- Enhances endurance, pain tolerance, and reaction speed through optimized Physical Magic flow.
- Creates a **visibly distinct combat form** that reflects the user’s physical identity.
- Causes **identity pressure**, where prolonged use may make the user feel more aligned with their Vessel form than their base body, which can cause severe problems when out of physical magic.
- Requires continuous Physical Magic maintenance while active; overuse leads to fatigue, muscle strain, and instability upon deactivation.

    1. Tall, lean, and highly muscular build optimized for agility and acrobatics.
    2. Enhanced climbing ability, aerial movement, and unpredictable combat flow

    3. Reflects his nurturing, protective, and nature-aligned combat style

3. **Bairon Jonathar Leorin** – _Apex Precision Frame_

    3. Reflects his controlled, calculated, and time-focused combat philosophy
