**Light magic** is said to be bestowed upon a person by the gods. It can be used for purifying things like curses, dormant dark magic cores, or Dreadmire/dark mist, light dark areas that then reveal secrets, healing injuries, or even buffing people in combat. It can be imbued into weapons and armor, and it also grants holy properties to elemental and physical magic. Mostly found in Humans because of their love for religion compared to other races. Light magic is stronger during the day, whereas dark magic is stronger at night; when an eclipse occurs, both light and dark are amplified.
