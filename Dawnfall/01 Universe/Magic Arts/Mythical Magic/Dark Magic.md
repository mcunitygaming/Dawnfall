**Dark magic** is by far the most unknown and uncommon magic type. It's the most hated by all races. If someone is born with this magic type, they would probably be executed or even dropped in no man's land. All demons can use it, and some Monsters can. Humans are the only other race that can be born with it, but only very rarely, like 1 in 5 million chance. When demons use dark magic, it produces a dark mist called Dreadmire, which is just dark-magic particles in the air. Dark magic is stronger at night, whereas light magic is stronger during the day; when an eclipse occurs, both light and dark are amplified. Dark magic can be bonded to all other magic types, and this will give whatever it's bonded to a huge power boost.

- **Physical magic:** When bonded with physical magic, you will get a huge power boost, allowing the user to do impossible feats, but without a strong consciousness, you can fall unconscious, and this will put you in a frenzy state; you won't be able to control yourself.
- **Elemental magic:** When bonded with elemental magic, it will create a dark variant of whatever it is connected to. After using a dark element magic spell, it will create Dreadmire.
- **Irregular magic:** When bonded with irregular magic, it becomes a curse or a dark variant of it. When bonded with Time magic, it will become a curse on age, either to age fast or die at a certain age. When bonded with Matter magic, the user can create far more powerful items that can cause curses on hit. Still, as a side effect, these items attract demons and monsters, along with the ability to nullify spells like an anti-magic. Dark magic can also be bonded to space magic to create a portal to a demon world or summon undead or a shadow troop.
