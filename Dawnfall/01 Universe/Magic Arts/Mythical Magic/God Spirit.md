**Symbolizes the non-physical spirit perfected**

God Spirits are **manifestations of a person’s highest potential and essence**, born when a living being achieves perfect harmony between their mind, soul, and magic. Unlike ordinary magic cores, God Spirits are **sentient, semi-autonomous entities** that bond with their host, amplifying abilities far beyond mortal limits. They are not merely tools—they are extensions of the user’s spirit, reflecting their deepest nature.

## Appearance

- God Spirits rarely appear fully in physical form; instead, they **radiate an aura that mirrors the user’s core personality and magic type**.
- Some may appear as **ethereal animals, elemental avatars, or abstract shapes**, often glowing faintly with colors unique to the user’s magic.
- When fully awakened, a God Spirit can **envelop its host in a manifestation of divine energy**, dramatically altering their appearance or aura (e.g., Cian’s totems glowing and pulsing with life, Bairon surrounded by an icy shimmer, Corv’s Hollow Conscious aura expanding with shadowy smoke).

## Function / Abilities

1. **Amplification of Magic**
    1. God Spirits enhance the user’s natural magic abilities, often **unlocking dormant potential or unique abilities** that ordinary magic cannot achieve.

2. **Synchronization with the Host**
    1. They respond to the user’s intentions and emotions, enabling **fluid, instinctive use of complex combinations of magic**.

3. **Partial Autonomy**
    1. Some God Spirits can act semi-independently to **protect or assist their host**, especially in critical situations.

4. **Risk Mitigation**
    1. For dangerous magic types like Dark or Time Magic, the God Spirit **stabilizes the flow of magic**, reducing the risk of self-harm or uncontrolled side effects.

## Requirements for Awakening

- **Extreme Emotional or Physical Trial:** The host must face a situation that pushes them beyond normal limits.
- **Inner Harmony / Acceptance:** The user must confront or accept an aspect of themselves—this could be fear, darkness, responsibility, or potential.
- **Magical Resonance:** The user must have sufficient mastery over their magic, often involving bonding multiple magic types or reaching a high level of skill.

## Effects on the Host

- Grants **immense magical power**, often enabling feats impossible for ordinary users.
- Enhances **physical, mental, and sensory capabilities** to superhuman levels.
- Often comes with **visible, thematic changes** reflecting the God Spirit’s nature (aura, eye color, smoke, elemental energy, or shadow-like features).
- Creates a **deep, lasting bond**: separation from the God Spirit can weaken or incapacitate the user.

## Examples of God Spirits in Aurvara

1. **Cian – The Verdant Stag**: Represents life, growth, and nature; unlocks Animal Totems. Linked to the Elemental Angels
2. **Bairon – The Kryha Arrow**: Represents precision, time, and control; unlocks Time + Ice magic binding. Linked to the Time Angel.
3. **Corv – The Conscious Abyss**: Represents perception, darkness, and self-mastery; unlocks Hollow Iris and stabilizes Dark Magic. Linked to the Dark Angel.
