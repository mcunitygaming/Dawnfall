**Liquid magic** is the control of **all liquids**. A liquid core is easy to create for chill, or go with the flow people. Siravell/Fish people are the most compatible with this phase. It is **mistaken for Water Magic** in Alpha and Aurvara due to its primitive magic knowledge.

## Water Magic
This is the mistook bounds of Liquid magic within Alpha and Aurvara.

### Water Spells

- **Tide Ripple** - A wave of condensed water pressure.
- **Water Lash** - Whips a string of water at high speeds.
- **Abyssal Pull** - Draws enemies or objects through currents like an undertow.
- **Mirrorwater** - Creates reflective illusions on water surfaces.
- **Deep Calm** - Nullifies emotional or magical turbulence.
