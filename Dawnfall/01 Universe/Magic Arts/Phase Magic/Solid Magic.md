**Solid magic** is the toughest and strongest. An solid core is easier to create in physically strong or steadfast people. Stone Kin/Dwarves are the most compatible with this phase. These mages will be seen as **Earth mages** in Alpha and Aurvara.

## Earth Magic
This is the mistook bounds of Solid magic within Alpha and Aurvara.

### Earth Spells

- **Stone Fist** - Grows a gauntlet of rock around the caster’s arm.
- **Terra Shield** - Raises a wall or dome of stone.
- **Stonebind** - Summons rock or stones to rap foes.
- **Gravel Shot** - Fires a cone of hardened stones.
- **Pulsequake** - Sends a localized tremor beneath enemies.
