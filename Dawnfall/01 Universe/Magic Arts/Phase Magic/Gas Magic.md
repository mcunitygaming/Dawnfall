This magic type allows a mage to control **all gases**. An gas core is created easily in pacifists and quiet people. **Gas magic** is the softest and most peaceful but can be the most deadly. Often Sylfen/Elves are the ones with this magic type. In Alpha and Aurvara the research of magic isn't developed enough to realize that the mage can control all gases, instead they will be mistaken as and named **Air Magic users**.
## Air Magic
This is the mistook bounds of Gas magic within Alpha and Aurvara.

### Air Spells

- **Gust Strike** - A slicing arc of compressed wind.
- **Zephyr Step** - Enhance movement speed with lightening winds.
- **Vortex Whorl** - A spinning wind pocket like a small tornado.
- **Skywhisper** - Sends messages carried through the air.
- **Vacuum Grip** – Creates a pocket of low air pressure, restricting movement.
