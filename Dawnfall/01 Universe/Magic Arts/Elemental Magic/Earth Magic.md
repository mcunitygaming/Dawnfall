**Earth magic** is the toughest and strongest element; it can create and move stones like a stone bullet and a stone wall. An earth core is easier to create in physically strong or larger people. Stone Kin/Dwarves are the most compatible with the element of earth.

## Earth Spells

- **Stone Fist** - Grows a gauntlet of rock around the caster’s arm.
- **Terra Shield** - Raises a wall or dome of stone.
- **Stonebind** - Summons rock or stones to rap foes.
- **Gravel Shot** - Fires a cone of hardened stones.
- **Pulsequake** - Sends a localized tremor beneath enemies.
