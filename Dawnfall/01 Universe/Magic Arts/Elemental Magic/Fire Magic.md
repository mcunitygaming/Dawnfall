**Fire magic** is by far the most destructive and chaotic, but once under control, it can be one of the most powerful. It has spells like fireball or flame thrower. A fire core is created easily in super extraverted people or someone with anger issues. Highborn Kin/Humans are the most compatible with the element of fire.

## Fire Spells

- **Ember Pulse** - A short-range burst of searing heat.
- **Phoenix Veil** - Surrounds the user with protective fire that burns projectiles.
- **Inferno Surge** - Calls forth explosive flames in an area.
- **Fireball** - A flaming hot ball that flies like a baseball.
- **Rocket Boots** - Fire condenses and explodes beneath users' feet, launching them into the air.
