**Air magic** is the softest and most peaceful. It can do things like wind-slice or even air-launch. An air core is created easily in pacifists and quiet people. Sylfen/Elves are the most compatible with the element of air.

## Air Spells

- **Gust Strike** - A slicing arc of compressed wind.
- **Zephyr Step** - Enhance movement speed with lightening winds.
- **Vortex Whorl** - A spinning wind pocket like a small tornado.
- **Skywhisper** - Sends messages carried through the air.
- **Vacuum Grip** – Creates a pocket of low air pressure, restricting movement.
