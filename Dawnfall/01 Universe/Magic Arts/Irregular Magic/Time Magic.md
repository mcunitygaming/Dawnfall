**Time magic** is perilous and by far the rarest form of magic (besides Dark magic in humans). The human body is not built to use time magic. People can see the future, but not too many times before they go blind. Another ability is to stop time for a brief moment. After one use, they are paralyzed for a week, and after the second use, they are paralyzed forever. They can master time magic by using it on objects (like growing plants or ageing wine) instead of themselves, and after mastering time magic, they can use it to be reborn again, once with all their memories. Time Magic is the only magic that can't be bonded with other magic types (except by Bairon Jonathar Leorin).
