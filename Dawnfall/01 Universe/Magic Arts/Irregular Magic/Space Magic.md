**Space magic** is the ability to travel through space, like portals or teleportation. A limit on the use of this magic, you are only able to travel to places you have been to or seen. This magic is split into two ways: imperfect and perfect. When space magic mixes with strong physical magic, it can become gravity magic. This works for both imperfect and perfect space magic.

- **Imperfect:** someone whose body does produce space magic, but at a deficient rate. For someone to use Space magic when they produce an imperfect version, they need to learn an elemental magic, and this will allow them to bond their magic to it. They cannot create portals or travel with multiple people. The only condition after bonding to use teleportation is that they are only allowed to teleport through the bonded element.
- **Perfect:** Someone born with perfect space magic, allowing them to create portals or travel instantly. They can use this magic without an Elemental Magic catalyst and even take as many people as possible, as long as a portal is open. If they master space magic, they can even create their own realm to portal from; also, if they master space magic, they can sacrifice themselves or their God Spirit to summon other things, like people, monsters, or items from other worlds.
