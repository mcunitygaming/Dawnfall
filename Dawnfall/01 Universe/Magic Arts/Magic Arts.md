Magic is an ancient mystery to the residents of Dawnfall. Particles of magic reside in a parallel world made only of these particles. Magic users can manipulate these particles. For example, a normal rock in the magic world looks like a clump of earth magic particles, which the mage can then change. Magic Arts differ for everyone; some possess unique colors or even magical powers and abilities. Some magic types are more compatible with different Races or different personalities. Magic, like Physical Magic, is something all living things are born with, while Irregular Magic is something you have to be born with. Elemental Magic, on the other hand, requires a core to be created by absorbing specific types of elemental particles. Lastly, there is Mythic Magic; nobody really knows how it is obtained except those who have it. People can have dormant magic types; this means they possess the core, but it won't develop and control the particles for reasons ranging from diseases to a mental block. All magic, except for time magic, can be mixed with other kinds, creating new sub-varieties or even special abilities. There is a form of elemental magic use called Uncontracted Magic, which is just using magic without a spell, allowing for free control over the magic, but it is almost impossible to use, and only Spellforged Mages, or higher, can use it; this does not apply to other magic types, as there are no spells to begin with for those. All magic power increases when the user is in tune with the same purpose and identity as the corresponding angel.

## Utilization

**Physical Magic:** particles will need to be produced from your core, then you will need to sense the magic in the core and make it flow throughout you, but once outside of the body, it's almost impossible to control. That's why people can only strengthen the object they are in contact with. To tame, you will need to weaken a hostile animal or earn the trust of a passive one. Then, direct contact is required to infuse your magic into the animal. If they are too weak to resist or voluntarily accept it, you will tame the animal. It also has Pure magic which just acts as a nonelement version of magic, so uncontracted and spell rules apply. Mental Magic is just physical magic but controlling someone else's physical magic causing mind control and hallucinations.

**Elemental Magic:** For the use of elemental magic, you will need to create a core first for the corresponding element. This will allow two different methods of use:

- **Uncontracted:** For the uncontracted method, it's the use of magic without a spell, allowing for free control over what it does, which can be very difficult to control, and almost nobody can use it.
- **Spells:** Spells, also known as contracted magic, are where a very talented mage comes along and uses uncontracted magic, then binds its movements to a spell, allowing for easy use by anyone.

Sub-variants of elemental magic can be used in the same way, but just by mixing the two elemental particles.

**Irregular Magic:** All magic that falls under this category has one method of use, and that is uncontracted magic. Luckily, Irregular cores are born fully structured — unlike elemental ones, this means that uncontracted magic is easier to use than elemental magic.

**Mythical Magic:** Acts like a mix of all other magic types as it has spells for light and dark magic but can be used like irregular magic easily. 
## Cores

All magic particles are in and formed in a parallel realm, while the magic cores, which are used to manipulate the particles, reside in both the living thing and the other realm.

- **Physical Magic** cores are born in all living things, from trees to people.
- **Elemental Magic** cores aren't born with; to form a core, you need to absorb surrounding particles in the other realm. (People in Dawnfall don't know you can absorb them if they try, so they view it as a natural talent thing)
- **Space Time and Matter Magic** cores can't be created because lower beings' bodies aren't made for it.
- **Light Magic** particles can be absorbed, but a core will not form unless given by a higher power, or so religion says.
- **Dark Magic** particles can be absorbed, which can either harm or kill them, or a dormant core that can only control and produce dark magic if a Devil Spirit is created; a high-level light magic user can cure the dormant core.

## Irregular Magic

**Irregular Magic** is all magic that stabilizes the universe. You have to be born with this form of magic: either [[Space Magic]], [[Time Magic]], or [[Matter Magic]]. This type of magic can be hazardous as it wasn't meant to be wielded by mortals.

## Elemental Magic

All elemental magic originates from four different World Trees: _World Tree of Fire, World Tree of Water, World Tree of Earth, World Tree of Air_. Originally, these magic types didn't mix in regions, but the trees were all burned down in the War of Dawn Fall. In the present day, the islands where the World Trees were are now dangerous, monster-infested islands. Magic is self-preserving, and this caused elemental magic types to bond with monsters, plants, or even animals. Nowadays, elemental magic particles are produced into the air all over the place. All living things can use this magic type by forming a core for the production of any element type. But in the world of Aurvara, magic information is very scarce. Most sorcerers create a core by unconsciously absorbing elemental magic-type particles. Each element has its own unique spells, with sub-variants like fireball, Flame frag and uses; many haven't even been discovered.

## Mythic Magic

This category of magic is all told in the most legendary stories. Mythic Magic is not a single type of magic, but a classification of phenomena that transcend standard magical systems, including Divine Magic (Light/Dark), and Identity States (Vessel Frame/God Spirits).
