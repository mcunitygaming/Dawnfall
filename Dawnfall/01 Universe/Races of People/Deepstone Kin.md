## Dwarves

- **Region:** Middle Eastern mountains
- **Traits:** Stoic, loyal, master craftsmen, protectors of the mountains
- **Magic Compatibility:** Earth Magic
- **Appearance:** Short, robust, built for mining and heavy labor
- **Notes:** Experts in forging, elemental earth manipulation, and material-based magic (perfect for Matter/Creation Magic).
- **Average Height:**
	- **Male:** 5 ft - 5 ft 2 in (152 cm - 157 cm)
	- **Female:** 4 ft 11 - 5 ft 1 in (149 cm - 154 cm)
