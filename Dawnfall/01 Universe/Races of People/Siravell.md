## Fish People

- **Region:** Northern seas
- **Traits:** Skilled in water magic, agile in water, mysterious traders, guardians of sacred seas
- **Magic Compatibility:** Water Magic
- **Appearance:** Aquatic form underwater with fins and scales; human-like form on land
- **Abilities:** Can switch between legs and a fishtail for optimal movement in water or land, Siravell are natural **anti-Dreadmire stabilizers** in ocean regions, explaining why seas are less corrupted.
- **Notes:** Rarely on land; their presence at Sunbreak Spire highlights the school’s mixed-race nature and allows underwater magic exploration.
- **Average Height:**
	- **Male:** 5 ft 6 in - 5 ft 8 in (167 cm - 172 cm)
	- **Female:** 5 ft 4 in - 5 ft 6 in (162 cm - 167 cm)
