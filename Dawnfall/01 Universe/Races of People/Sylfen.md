## Elves

- **Region:** Western forests, in colossal living trees
- **Traits:** Wise, long-lived, graceful, quiet, and intense
- **Magic Compatibility:** Air Magic
- **Appearance:** Pointed-eared, humanoid, ethereal beauty due to long lifespans
- **Notes:** Often act as guardians of natural forces; highly compatible with aerial and wind-based magic training.
- **Average Height:**
	- **Male:** 6 ft 2 in - 6 ft 7 in (187 cm - 200 cm)
	- **Female:** 6 ft - 6 ft 6 in (182 cm - 198 cm)
