## Humans

- **Region:** Dawnlands (plains and sunlit fields)
- **Traits:** Adaptable, ambitious, culturally rich, guided by religion and noble houses (e.g., House Highcrest)
- **Magic Compatibility:** Fire Magic (passion, aggression, ambition)
- **Appearance:** Standard human, versatile depending on region and class
- **Notes:** Humans are the most hated of all races due to their chaotic nature and are the most populous population in the whole world.
- **Average Height:**
	- **Male:** 5 ft 6 in - 5 ft 8 in (167 cm - 172 cm)
	- **Female:** 5 ft 4 in - 5 ft 6 in (162 cm - 167 cm)
