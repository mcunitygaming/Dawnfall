There are 4 base realms, along with all the others owned by perfect space magic users.

- Dawnfall Realm
    - This realm is physical and contains normal planets, life, and physical magic.
- Magic Realm
    - The Magic realm is a semi-physical realm. It acts as a ghost realm of the Dawnfall realm, in which if something changes in the Magic realm, it also changes in the Dawnfall realm. The only matter that exists in here is magic particles and the dark angel.
- Angel Realm
    - The Angel realm is completely non-physical and contains all angels except the dark angel. It's also known as heaven and is the only realm that will last for eternity.
- Thoughtborn Realm
    - This realm is where the elven hero of legend imprisoned the evil ones, also known as the **Thoughtborn**, along with the creation machine. **The Thoughtborn Realm** is a nightmare dimension created and shaped by the collective thoughts of countless minds. It appears as an endless mountain rainforest wrapped in eternal night, with scattered cities, floating islands, and impossible landscapes shifting throughout the world. Thick fog covers much of the land, hiding creatures known as Thoughtborn—manifestations of fear, obsession, and corrupted imagination. Those dragged into the realm are constantly hunted, not just physically but mentally, as the Thoughtborn seek to break their will and reshape their minds. At the center of the realm lies the Creation Machine, an ancient construct responsible for sustaining the dimension and creating the horrors that inhabit it.
