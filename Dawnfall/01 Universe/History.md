The world is governed by 10 angels that each embody an aspect of reality, this is explained in the [[Cosmology]] note

Originally, a single human faction discovered the magic particle realm and began drawing power from it, enabling the use of magic. They eventually created a machine that turned thoughts into physical entities; however, the device malfunctioned due to the dark nature of human thoughts. In response, wealthy humans fled to a different solar system, losing the location of their origin planet in the process.

The poor humans left behind on the origin planet split into several groups:

- **The Elves**: One faction initially kidnapped and experimented on others, creating the first "beastmen." This group eventually repented and turned toward peace, transforming over time into the elves, who developed long ears from listening to nature and gained extended lifespans.
- **The Dwarves**: Another group settled beneath the mountains to create tools to help the beastmen. Due to the high underground air pressure, they grew smaller in stature.
- **The Wasteland Humans**: The remaining humans left the continent to explore the world's wastelands, eventually returning much later.

Over time, the bodies of the beastmen adapted to their changes while the other races evolved into their distinct forms.

## [[Game Story]]
Following the human split and evolution, the wasteland humans returned after realizing the outside world was uninhabitable. Their return sparked a war for land, as their home continent was the **only place with lush vegetation**. During this time, **an elf was banished** from their tribe for killing an attacker in defense of their family.

As the story progresses, ancient high-tech weapons and tools are used to burn the world's trees and destroy historical records, reverting the world to a **mixed medieval culture**. The banished elf eventually travels the globe, ends the war, and seals the imagination machine in one of the [[Realms]] known as **the thoughtborn realm** and even explored the [[World Trees]]. He becomes the first of four individuals to reach the "Ascendant" power level, fulfilling a prophecy that **one hero has arrived and three are yet to come**.
