## Description

Noctyra is a legendary sword known as the Soul Mirror Blade. It's a dark, unexplained blade that reflects the soul of its wielder, forming the perfect weapon for whoever uses it. 

It is Owned by [[Corvelan]] currently and was originally wielded by the elven warriors companion explained in the [[Game Story]] note

---

## Corvelans Forms
Corvelan finds the Noctyra in his time in the Thoughtborn realm laying in front of the entrance to the room holding the Imagination Machine.

### Armor Form

When Corvelan isn't using Noctyra to fight, he will force it to take the form of rings or things that attach all over his body, then he'd raise the weight of it, causing strain on his body, making even slight movements a workout

### Weapon Form

Longsword x Nodachi mix

- **Blade Shape:** Fully straight, double-edged blade — merging a Lowgswords blade with a Nodachi’s imposing length. The symmetry enhances balance and allows cutting from both sides.
- **Length:** Approximately 180 cm/6ft, maintaining a Nodachi’s extended reach.
- **Blade Profile:** Double-edged with a central ridge running the length of the blade for strength and rigidity. Both edges are sharpened to a fine, mirror-polished finish for lethal efficiency in slashing and thrusting.
- **Tang and Handle:** Full tang with a long tsuka designed for two-handed use, wrapped in ray skin and silk cord for secure handling.
- **Guard (Tsuba):** Minimalist oval or diamond-shaped guard, offering hand protection without adding unnecessary weight.
- **Balance:** Perfectly centered by the double-edged design, enabling fluid transitions between offensive and defensive movements.
- **Aesthetic:** Polished steel with a subtle hamon-like temper line along both edges, symbolizing duality and mastery.
