## Description

Noctyra is a legendary sword known as the Soul Mirror Blade. It's a dark, unexplained blade that reflects the soul of its wielder, forming the perfect weapon for whoever uses it. 

This weapon is one of the creations of the Formation Engine before the events of Alpha.

Ownership
1. Formation Engine Scientist
	- Created from an illegal test of the Form E
2. [[1 Alpha]] Protagonist's companion
	- Unknown
3. Corvelan
	- Found it around the center of the Thoughtborn Realm near the Formation Engine 
4. Caelen
	- Acquired from Corv as a gift for reincarnation 

## Corvelan's Forms

### Armor Form

When Corvelan isn't using Noctyra to fight, he will force it to take the form of rings or things that attach all over his body, then he'd raise the weight of it, causing strain on his body, making even slight movements a workout

### Weapon Form

Longsword x Nodachi mix

- **Blade Shape:** Fully straight, double-edged blade — merging a Longswords blade with a Nodachi’s imposing length. The symmetry enhances balance and allows cutting from both sides.
- **Length:** Approximately 180 cm/6ft, maintaining a Nodachi’s extended reach.
- **Blade Profile:** Double-edged with a central ridge running the length of the blade for strength and rigidity. Both edges are sharpened to a fine, mirror-polished finish for lethal efficiency in slashing and thrusting.
- **Tang and Handle:** Full tang with a long tsuka designed for two-handed use, wrapped in ray skin and silk cord for secure handling.
- **Guard:** Minimalist oval or diamond-shaped guard, offering hand protection without adding unnecessary weight.
- **Balance:** Perfectly centered by the double-edged design, enabling fluid transitions between offensive and defensive movements.
- **Aesthetic:** Polished steel with a subtle hamon-like temper line along both edges, symbolizing duality and mastery.

## Caelen Forms
### Base Form
This item takes the shape of a liquid plated metal left arm that Caelen uses to replace his lost one. The liquid metal can be moved on top of his body all throughout creating a living armor that will auto react and protect him from harm. The metal plating can be used to form a sword or two daggers that Caelen can use when battling. Any material that has been lost or thrown can and will find a way back to merge with the original piece.
