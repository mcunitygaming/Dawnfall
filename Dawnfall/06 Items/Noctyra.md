## Description

Noctyra is a legendary item known as a Soul Mirror. It's a dark, unexplained item that reflects the soul of its wielder, forming the perfect tool for whoever uses it. 

This item is one of the creations of the Formation Engine before the events of Alpha.

Ownership
1. [[Doctor]]
	- Created from an illegal test of the Form E
2. [[Mercy]]
	- Unknown how he obtained it
3. [[Corvelan]]
	- Found it around the center of the Thoughtborn Realm near the Formation Engine 
4. [[Connor-Caelen]]
	- Acquired from Corv as a gift for reincarnation 

## Mercy's Forms

### Armor Form
When in this form Mercy will be partly covered in armor plating like [Camille Arno](https://endfield.wiki.gg/wiki/Camille). He also wears it as a [warden helmet](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcTt7VzZd_ytlo3Q4XLbRw-D1xm4q1JC6_bXhG_sZyCqVg&s=10) with a hood up and over it.

Unlike other owners when Mercy changes the form to a weapon his armor doesn't go away. in fact it looks like Mercy just pulls a sword out of any one of the armor pieces he wears. 

### Weapon Form
Noctyra takes this form when ever hostile intent is directed toward Mercy.

## Corvelan's Forms

### Armor Form
When Corvelan isn't using Noctyra to fight, he will force it to take the form of rings or things that attach all over his body, then he would increase the weight of, causing strain on his body, making even slight movements a workout

### Weapon Form
Longsword x Nodachi mix

- **Blade Shape:** Fully straight, double-edged blade — merging a Longswords blade with a Nodachi’s imposing length and guard.
- **Length:** Approximately 180 cm/6ft, maintaining a Nodachi’s extended reach.
- **Blade Profile:** Double-edged with a central ridge running the length of the blade. Both edges are sharpened to a fine, mirror-polished finish.
- **Tang and Handle:** Full tang with a long tsuka designed for two-handed use, wrapped in ray skin and silk cord.
- **Guard:** Minimalist oval or diamond-shaped guard

## Caelen's Forms
### Base Form
This item takes the shape of a liquid plated metal left arm that Caelen uses to replace his lost one. The liquid metal can be moved on top of his body all throughout creating a living armor that will auto react and protect him from harm. The metal plating can be used to form a sword or two daggers that Caelen can use when battling. Any material that has been lost or thrown can and will find a way back to merge with the original piece.
