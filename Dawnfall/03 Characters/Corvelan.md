## Personality Info

Corvelan is quiet, independent, and selectively motivated. He rarely seeks attention, not because he dislikes people, but because he sees little reason to involve himself unless something genuinely interests him. He naturally weighs effort against outcome, often without realizing it, and tends to reserve his energy for things he personally considers worthwhile. This can make him appear lazy to others, though in reality, he is highly capable and simply refuses to expend effort without purpose.

Despite his reserved nature, Corv is not shy. He is perfectly comfortable speaking to others when necessary and has little concern for how people perceive him. After using Hollow Iris, for example, he often walks around with no eyes beneath Argentum. The mask exists only because the school staff insists upon it; if it were up to him, he would hardly care who saw. In fact, he occasionally finds people's reactions amusing and is known to use his appearance to unsettle those around him.

Corv is exceptionally observant. Rather than focusing on a single detail, he constantly cycles through sounds, movement, positioning, expressions, and tone, assembling a complete picture from fragments. He often appears distracted during conversations, yet he is almost always listening while simultaneously watching something else. Because of this, he frequently understands situations faster than people expect, choosing to keep that understanding to himself.

He learns through observation, experience, and experimentation, rapidly shifting between them rather than focusing on one method. This allows him to adapt quickly and absorb information with surprising efficiency. However, he rarely feels the need to prove what he knows, often holding back unless circumstances require otherwise.

A lifelong climber, Corv instinctively gravitates toward rooftops, rafters, trees, and other elevated places. Heights feel natural to him, providing both comfort and awareness without forcing him into the center of attention. This habit influences both his daily life and combat style, giving him a fluid, predatory way of moving that many compare more to an animal than a person.

Corv processes the world through observation before emotion. When faced with pressure, he rarely reacts immediately, preferring to watch, think, and understand before acting. Even then, he usually does only what is necessary. Though he often appears distant, he possesses a quiet kindness expressed through dry humor, subtle acts of protection, and an uncanny ability to remember small details about the people around him.

He rarely initiates relationships, friendships, or conversations, content to let life unfold without his involvement. Yet once someone becomes important to him, his loyalty is unwavering. Trust, to Corv, is not something spoken aloud but something demonstrated through actions. While he struggles to understand and express his feelings, he instinctively notices the habits, emotions, and needs of those he cares about, often remembering things about them that even they have forgotten.

After meeting Halira, this aspect of him begins to change. What starts as simple curiosity gradually becomes genuine care, then responsibility. Through her influence, Corv slowly learns that caring for others is not a weakness or a burden. Over time, that care expands from one person to a small group, and eventually to the entire world. His growth is not defined by becoming stronger, but by choosing that some things—and some people—are worth the effort.

### God Spirit - The Conscious Abyss

Represents perception, darkness, and self-mastery; it unlocks Hollow Iris and stabilizes Dark Magic. He gained this spirit after his third year at Sunbreak Spire.

**Hollow Iris** is an ability that enhances all senses to an **Ascendant level of perception**. When using this ability, his eyes disappear and are replaced with faintly glowing purple smoke that spills out from his eye sockets for around half a foot of distance. The smoke that appears from his eye socket is actually _physical magic particles_ that eventually become invisible and fly through the space he occupies, giving him that insane level of perception, even entering living things, allowing him to see their _emotions and thoughts_. His eyes will form back after a full week; that's why he covers his eyes with his **Argentum**.

**Hollow Conscious** forms from Corvelan, getting angry with his God Spirit active like a devil spirit, but instead of losing himself completely, he can turn back after calming down. It is just an upgraded version that adds **enhanced physical abilities along with the god-like perception**. This form appears as a shadow of him or a slender man's skin, but black. His face will have smoke coming out of his eyes, as Hollow Iris does, but this time, he also has a demonic smile that emits smoke. Corv creates this form by mixing **Hollow Iris and his dormant dark magic**. It will then flow around in his body and spill out as a strong, glowing purple smoke from his eyes and mouth. That makes it so that he has **immense regenerative ability** to the point of being able to survive from just his pool of blood, which means that after using this form, his body will return to normal, fully healed (no missing eyes). This form also splits his personality into two different people, **the Hunter side and the Executioner side**. The Hunter side with messing with and scaring people he is attacking, for example, he'll let them run just to cut them off, inducing intense fear into his opponents if the Hunter side loses interest through the opponent being composed or just being too strong his personality will switch to the Executioner side when he is in this mode Corvelan with lose all attachment to things in his life causing him to stop at nothing to not just kill but mutilate his enemies.

### Combat Style

Corv created this style from his childlike obsession with climbing. He'd often be found in high locations and could, unconsciously, note weaknesses in the areas he'd climb, which also carried over into his fights, allowing him to identify enemy weaknesses quickly.

Corvelan used a technique that he found in his time in the thoughtborn realm, where instead of using conduit organs to strengthen weapons, the person cuts open themselves and places the organ in their own body, then fuses it to them, gaining power.

#### Philosophy & Mindset

_Corvelan's style embodies_ the predator’s instinct fused with chaotic spontaneity. He seeks to overwhelm his opponents not just with skill, but with unpredictability. Corv embraces fearlessness and unpredictability, seeing chaos as a weapon. Mental discipline is paradoxically required to **remain controlled while appearing unhinged**.

#### Magic Uses

- **Fire Magic**
    - **Sun's Child:** Corv will condense as much fire magic as possible into a small orb that he will then punch something with. After impact, the orb will explode outward, dealing immense damage. Although this technique is powerful, it is also incredibly dangerous, as the explosion can fling him back or injure his limbs. Since Corv spends a good chunk of his time fighting in the air, if he prepares a sun's child and gets caught off guard in the air, he will use it to propel him to another direction.
    
    - **Purest Functions:** He can superheat and pressurize his muscles to produce insane, explosive muscle contractions, allowing for super-strength feats even without physical magic. With this technique, Corv can also heat his nerves, giving him temporary superhuman reflexes, or even heat his blood and cells to enhance healing and burn toxins/poisons. Although he can heal faster, it takes extreme consecration, so he's only able to use this ability after or outside of combat. The costs to this can be things like muscle tearing, severe dehydration, internal bleeding if overused, nerve damage, and even temporary paralysis if pushed too far.
    
    - **World's Furnace:** Corvelan condenses fire magic into the air surrounding a target, subtly increasing thermal pressure and oxygen strain. Though not hot enough to burn, the heightened heat triggers the body’s primal survival instincts, accelerating heart rate and inducing involuntary fear responses. Victims experience sweating, tremors, and an overwhelming sense of danger, as if standing before an unseen wildfire. The effect intensifies with Corv’s focus and emotional state. Strong-willed individuals can resist the panic, but even they feel the weight of his presence. This ability is not only used by him for fear, but Corvelan would also warm the air to make the people he trusts comfortable.
