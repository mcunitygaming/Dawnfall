## Personality info

Cian is a nice and thoughtful person, as shown by his care for others. You would often see him carving little toys for people and, in some cases, cooking for them. He loves the outdoors, especially walking through beautiful landscapes, whether it's with or without someone to chat with. These enjoyments Cian has stemmed from his social farming family. Cian has an acute sense of smell, which helped fuel his love for cooking, and an amazing ability to hear.

### God Spirit - The Verdant Stag

Represents life, growth, and nature; it unlocks Animal Totems. He mixes his plant and creation magic to make animal totems that he hangs from his antlers. When he uses Physical Magic on them and then crushes the totem, he will gain the animal's powers for a bit. Example: a bird totem will give him wings.

#### Rules / Limitations for Totems

1. **Activation:**
    1. Physical Magic is required when crushing a totem to gain the ability.
    2. Only **one ability can be active at once** to avoid extreme fatigue.

2. **Preparation:**
    1. Totems must be **crafted with wood + Creation Magic**.
    2. They can be **lost, stolen, or destroyed**, limiting him in combat.

3. **Fatigue:**
    1. Using stronger animals (Bear, Deer, or multiple simultaneously) **drains stamina or Physical Magic reserves**.
    2. Overuse risks exhaustion, temporary unconsciousness, or lessened effectiveness.

#### Advanced / Legendary Totems

- **Griffon / Ziz:** Flight + moderate strength + great perception + weaker animal control. Duration: 1–2 minutes.
- **Behemoth / Giant Beast Totem:** Extreme strength + size + durability. Duration: 30–45 seconds.
- **Phoenix Totem:** Magic resistance + regeneration. Duration: 1 minute.

### Combat Style

Cian's combat style is focused on where others on his team lack, often carving totems beforehand based on their weaknesses, and when the time arises, he will support his team. When alone, his fights can be fought from any distance, whether it's long-distance fighting with his leather sling and some rocks from his earth magic or close-range with his trusty pitchfork. His totems are used mostly on his own weak points; they are rarely used to boost his attributes when his base abilities are enough.
