This ability comes in two forms **Consternation** and **Trepidation**.

### Consternation
This part of Tangible Dread causes **smoke to spill from Caelen’s body** into the surrounding area, similar to Corvelan’s ability. However, unlike Corvelan, whose eyes are the sole source of the smoke, Caelen’s **entire body emits it**. Furthermore, instead of the smoke gathering information about his surroundings, Tangible Dread inflicts overwhelming fear on anyone around. This fear doesn't just apply to living things; he can even scare the essence of anything, causing items like cameras to fail to record him, either showing static or as if he was never in the camera's view. This aspect can paralyze almost everyone and anyone it doesn't wont be able to move for very long.

### Trepidation
The ability works a little different when Caelen uses Tangible Dread like this. instead of the smoke filling the room/area Caelen's in the fear will seep into and flood a single persons body. When used like this the dread is so concentrated and great that it can cause people to just die on contact, even if someone has training or an innate talent for fearlessness, the smoke is so strong it pops cells, causing immense pain and blood to spill from the victim's body along with flooding the brain with visions. Victims describe it like your brain is being stuffed with infinite information, but you're still able to feel each individual pain receptor in your body. All survivors leave with some form of disability

### Combat Style
Connor has practiced many versions of martial arts for example: Taekwondo, Boxing, Karate, Wrestling, Jujitsu, and basic self defense. All of this transfers over to Caelen within his unarmed combat style. Caelen is rarely ever unarmed though as he uses his environment to its fullest, but even if he doesn't Caelen still has Noctyra which can form a shattered long sword for him to use.
