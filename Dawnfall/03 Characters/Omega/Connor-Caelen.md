### God Spirit - Tangible Dread

Tangible Dread causes smoke to spill from Caelen’s body into the surrounding area, similar to Corvelan’s ability. However, unlike Corvelan, whose eyes are the sole source of the smoke, Caelen’s entire body emits it. Furthermore, instead of the smoke gathering information about his surroundings, Tangible Dread inflicts overwhelming fear on anyone Caelen chooses. This fear doesn't just apply to living things; he can even scare the essence of anything, causing items like cameras to fail to record him, either showing static or as if he was never in the camera's view. The fear is so great that it can cause anyone untrained to just die on contact when at 100%, but even if someone has training or has an innate talent for fearlessness, the dread is so strong it can pop cells, causing immense pain and blood to spill from the victim's body.

### Combat Style
Connor has practiced many versions of martial arts for example: Taekwondo, Boxing, Karate, Wrestling, Jujitsu, and basic self defense. All of this transfers over to Caelen within his unarmed combat style. Caelen is rarely ever unarmed though as he uses his environment to its fullest, but even if he doesn't Caelen still has Noctyra which can form a shattered long sword for him to use.
