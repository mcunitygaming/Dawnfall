## Personality info

The Doctor was a very passionate man stiving any steps to reach his goals, and this gave him his nickname "The Evilest Hero". Often, the only location people saw the Doctor in was his laboratory. He is one of the creators of the Formation Engine[^1] and the first person to discover the magic realm[^2] . Originally the researchers created it to help develop society, but the Doctor strived for humanity to have power over all creation. This led him to create [[Noctyra]] from illiegal tests he was doing on Form E.
