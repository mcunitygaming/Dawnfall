## Personality info

"People always assume I'm shy.

I'm not.

I'm just... comfortable being alone.

If I'm by myself, I'm perfectly happy staying that way. I'll put on some music, mess with some tech, wander around the city, whatever. If nobody talks to me all day, I won't even notice.

But if somebody drags me out of that? It's like a switch flips.

Then suddenly I'm talking, joking around, and before long I'm asking if everyone wants to hang out again tomorrow.

It's weird.

People think introverts hate people and extroverts hate being alone. I'm neither. I just don't like being the one to start things.

Back on Earth, my cousins figured that out before I did. They'd text me asking to hang out, and I'd always tell them, "No."

Then they'd show up at my house anyway.

Five hours later I'd be asking if they wanted to stay the night.

...

School was even funnier.

Apparently there were people who thought I was mute.

I slept through half my classes, wore my hood up, and if someone I didn't know talked to me, they'd usually get a grunt or a nod. A couple people told me they'd gone months without hearing my actual voice.

The teachers weren't much better. Most days I don't think they even realized I was there unless they called on me.

It's not that I dislike people.

I just don't naturally walk toward them.

If you walk toward me, though...

Well...

You're probably stuck with me."
