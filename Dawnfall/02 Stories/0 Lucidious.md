This is a horror story I created when I was a kid and want to write down these ideas before they are lost.

## Chapter 1
In this chapter the main character, named Gus, starts his adventure into darker realms as a child ~8 years old.

**Dream:** This will be a tutorial/introduction to the game. The dream will be a happy and joyful world that Gus will go through.

**Day:** Gus wakes up in school with the teacher telling everyone its time for recess, but when he goes outside Gus remembers a toy he lost. He ends up going into the forest in search of his belonging. There he sees lucidious (The Monster) who will force him into a dream putting him unconscious.

**Dream 2:** In this dream, Gus wakes up in a forest on an island as an adult. He will build and explore the island where Gus will encounter cannibals and monsters he will have to fend off and in these struggles he will find a pedestal. Gus will investigate this pedestal putting his arm on its top and when leaning around it he won't notice a liquid crawls up his arm forming a wrist blade. he realizes too late and feels a intense pain like something is replacing everything in his left forearm. This wrist blade isn't a weapon though its actually a key he can extend or retract out of his arm.

**Day 2:** Gus after going through the rest of his dream events will wake and find he is a kid again but still has the key blade even in real life.

## Chapter 2
This chapter Gus is now a young adult going through his normal life but still having these dreams of horror. As a young adult Gus took it upon himself to learn how to defend himself, learning many different types of martial arts to help him fight off creatures.

**Dream:** When going through this dream Gus will wake in an apocalyptic, monster, and zombie infested city. He will find a liminal mall basement, and when exploring he will find more pedestals. On said pedestals instead of more wrist blades there will be a glowing stone that he will notice a gem slot in his wrist blade where the stones will slot into. Installing said gems will grant him abilities he'll use on his adventures.

**Day:** Waking up, Gus will find he is in the old island from his first dream again.
