This story follows the descendants of the wealthy humans who abandoned Dawnfall after the catastrophe of the Imagination Creation Machine explained in the [[History]] note.

After living full lives, **Corvelan, Bairon, and Cian** die of old age and inherit the roles of the **Space, Time, and Matter Angels**, respectively. As the new Space Angel, Corvelan discovers the other half of his soul living on Earth—a sixteen-year-old boy named **Connor**.

Rather than reincarnating Connor into Dawnfall, Corvelan grants him a unique gift. Whenever Connor falls unconscious, he can freely travel between **Earth** and **Dawnfall**. Time completely pauses in whichever world he leaves, allowing him to live two separate lives without losing either one. Corvelan also allows Connor to choose one other person to share this gift.

Connor chooses his cousin, **Byron**, who unknowingly carries the other half of Bairon's soul.

Although Connor and Byron remain sixteen years old on Earth, they are born as infants in Dawnfall and must grow up naturally there, experiencing an entirely separate lifetime. As they mature, they slowly uncover the history of humanity's forgotten origin, the civilization that fled the Imagination Creation Machine, and the mysteries still surrounding Dawnfall itself.

Unlike _Aurvara_, this story takes place among the descendants of the humans who escaped the original planet. Their civilization spans entire star systems and features towering cyberpunk megacities, advanced space travel, and technology far beyond what remains on Dawnfall.

The result is a science-fiction adventure with elements inspired by **Star Wars**, **Cyberpunk**, **Gargantia on the Verdurous Planet**, and **Milky☆Subway**, while still remaining deeply connected to the history and mythology established in _Aurvara_.
