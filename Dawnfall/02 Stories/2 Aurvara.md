# Story Arcs

Three protagonists, **Corvelan, Cian, and Bairon**, each fundamentally incomplete in different ways, must synchronize their distinct ways of understanding reality to stabilize a collapsing world system. **_3 people 3 problems 1 ending_**.

**Emotional Development:**

- **Bairon**: starts 2nd → ends 1st (earliest completion)
- **Cian**: starts 3rd → ends 2nd (mid completion)
- **Corv**: starts 1st → ends last (latest completion, slow burn)

There is a "**Soul-Link**" transition in the school seasons that functions as a seamless narrative bridge between episodes in a **12-episode** season, in which the three main characters each receive four dedicated POV episodes in a rotating cycle of **Bairon -> Cian -> Corvelan**, then repeat. The transition begins at the end of an episode, shifting from a 3rd-person view of Bairon to a 1st-person perspective as the camera "hits" the back of his head, then fades to black as he closes his eyes. The next episode opens in total darkness, then Cian’s eyes snap open in 1st-person; as he sits up and moves, the camera pulls out from the back of his head, returning to a 3rd-person view, signaling the start of his narrative arc.

## Opening / Ending Monologue

“Space. Time. Matter.”
-- **Corvelan**

“Three aspects that must exist together.”
-- **Bairon**

“Completely dependent on one another… and yet entirely different.”
-- **Cian**

“People often misunderstand them, because each has both a physical meaning… and one beyond that.”
-- **All Three**

“This world has distance, so things can exist apart from one another.

Space doesn't play favorites.

It makes room for cruelty just as easily as kindness.

Evil exists because the world allows space for it to exist… but also righteousness.

In the end, good is only good when its chosen.”
-- **Corvelan**

“People dream about reliving moments… resetting mistakes… stopping time before something important disappears.

But time moves for everyone.

It carries away the good with the bad, joy with grief, whether we’re ready or not.

So when life drags you through the mud…

Crawl and when you get the chance stand and keep walking, or you'll be left behind.”
-- **Bairon**

“A single object can become a gift for someone you love…

or a weapon that harms another.

The physical world is never yours alone, only everyone's.

It’s easy to dedicate your death to someone and say "I'll die for you".

The harder action is this:

Are you willing to dedicate your life to them?”
-- **Cian**
