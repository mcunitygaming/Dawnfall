Magic is an ancient mystery to the residents of Dawnfall. Particles of magic reside in a parallel world made only of these particles. Magic users can manipulate these particles causing ancient humans to find the realm. For example, a normal rock in the magic world looks like a clump of earth magic particles, which the mage can then change. Magic Arts differ for everyone; some possess unique colors or even magical powers and abilities. Some magic types are more compatible with different Races or different personalities. Magic, like Physical Magic, is something all living things are born with, while Irregular Magic is something you have to be born with. Elemental Magic, on the other hand, requires a core to be created by absorbing specific types of particles. Lastly, there is Mythic Magic; nobody really knows how it is obtained except those who have it. People can have dormant magic types; this means they possess the core, but it won't develop and control the particles for reasons ranging from diseases to a mental block. All magic, except for time magic, can be mixed with other kinds, creating new sub-varieties or even special abilities. There is a form of phase magic use called Uncontracted Magic, which is just using magic without a spell, allowing for free control over the magic, but it is almost impossible to use, and only Spellforged Mages, or higher, can use it; this does not apply to other magic types, as there are no spells to begin with for those. All magic power increases when the user is in tune with the same purpose and identity as the corresponding angel. 

## Origins
All magic comes from a parallel realm which was discovered by ancient humans before the exodus covered in the History note. These old time humans created four world trees to allow these magic particles to bleed into the main realm. unfortunately these world trees were burned to the ground during the War of Dawn Fall. Luckily, magic is self-preserving, and this caused magic types to bond with monsters, plants, or even animals. Nowadays, magic particles are produced into the air all over the place on the origin planet. 

## Utilization

**Physical Magic:** particles will need to be produced from your core, then you will need to sense the magic in the core and make it flow throughout you, but once outside of the body, it's almost impossible to control. That's why people can only strengthen the object they are in contact with. To tame, you will need to weaken a hostile animal or earn the trust of a passive one. Then, direct contact is required to infuse your magic into the animal. If they are too weak to resist or voluntarily accept it, you will tame the animal. It also has Pure magic which just acts as a nonelement version of magic, so uncontracted and spell rules apply. Mental Magic is just physical magic but controlling someone else's physical magic causing mind control and hallucinations.

**Phase Magic:** For the use of this magic, you will need to create a core first for the corresponding phase. This will allow two different methods of use:

- **Uncontracted:** For the uncontracted method, it's the use of magic without a spell, allowing for free control over what it does, which can be very difficult to control, and almost nobody can use it.
- **Spells:** Spells, also known as contracted magic, are where a very talented mage comes along and uses uncontracted magic, then binds its movements to a spell, allowing for easy use by anyone.

**Irregular Magic:** All magic that falls under this category has one method of use, and that is uncontracted magic. Luckily, Irregular cores are born fully structured — unlike elemental ones, this means that uncontracted magic is easier to use than elemental magic.

**Mythical Magic:** Acts like a mix of all other magic types as it has spells for light and dark magic but can be used like irregular magic easily. 

### Specials
**Covenant of Growth:** Beings can offer items of importance to progress further in Magic Arts. (This will lead to the sacrifice of life with a soul causing corruption by Thoughtborn) Beings can pledge themselves or their freedom to a specific magic type to **double its power**, but becoming **twice as worse in all other magic variants**. A person can even make this pledge for a specific reason, for example destruction and this will force all impulses and abilities in that region to be enhanced. If this pledge is broken the user will **lose all control of the magic they pledged to** and will never be able to form their God spirit. The only way to break a pledge is to go to one of the world trees and destroy the **Covenant of Growth** in the temple at the middle (It does not matter what one of the World Trees they go to and sacrificed things can not be brought back).
