In **Dawnfall**, the supreme creator is the **Christian God** — the singular origin of all existence. From Him came the **Angels**, each embodying a fundamental law of reality whether **Space, Time, and Matter**. These Angels are not “gods” by creation, but function as divine administrators of the world leading them to be worshiped as such (Mindism). It is known that Angels blessed the World Trees to bring magic into this world.

They do not typically interfere directly. Instead, their influence manifests through:
- Magic Arts
- Reality Structure
- Elements
- Deities Gene
- Covenant of Growth

## Space Angel
This Angel is said to hold all the space that matter occupies together. Governs distance, position, darkness, and the unknown. Without it, matter would drift apart and/or collapse inward. The most mysterious Angel in doctrine.

**Domain(s):**
- Space
- Secrecy
- Dark
- Illusion
## Time Angel
This Angel is the one who pushes existence forward. Governs causality, aging, light, free will, and progression. Time cannot be stopped — only redirected or strained. Although it does see all of time simultaneously, it exists within the time it's moving. There is only one time angel that has ever existed know as "Bairon".

## Matter Angel

- **Matter Angel**
This Angel created the structure of reality and the other Angels. Governs Creation Magic and structural transmutation. God created this angel first and then, through it, created the other angels, but this does not put it in a position of power, as it can only create, not destroy.
