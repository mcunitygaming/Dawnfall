## **Monocruph** – A Shades Predator

## Overview

- **Type:** Shadow/Spirit Beast
- **Realm:** Exists partially in its own shadow plane—a parallel pocket-realm where it can move freely and observe the physical world simultaneously. This realm overlaps with the material plane, allowing it to phase in and out at will.
- **Role:** They float around and only attack if a dark magic core in humans or beasts is present.

### Appearance

- **Form:** Made of dark smoke, but usually forms into a sleek, humanoid-cat hybrid shape, bipedal or quadrupedal depending on hunting mode. No fur—its body is smooth, almost obsidian, reflecting faint traces of ambient light.
- **Eyes:** Glowing, like small lanterns, or sometimes just voids of black.
- **Limbs:** Long, jointed limbs ending in clawed fingers/toes. Agile, able to leap incredible distances.
- **Tail:** Semi-liquid, wisp-like shadow tail that can stretch to manipulate objects or distract prey.
- **Aura:** Constant thin haze of shadow particles (like Dreadmire) drifting from its body.

### Abilities

1. **Phasing / Realm-Walking:**
    1. Can partially leave the physical plane to move through its own shadow realm.
    2. Can attack or observe from the shadow realm while mostly intangible in the material world.

2. **Predatory Perception:**
    1. Senses **emotional or magical signatures** of nearby creatures through the shadow realm.
    2. Can track targets by detecting dark magic cores or strong magic.

3. **Dark Magic Affinity:**
    1. Naturally attracts dark magic. Can accelerate the formation of a **Devil Spirit** in nearby weak-willed beings.

4. **Ethereal Speed & Reflexes:**
    1. Capable of moving faster than the eye can track.
    2. Can flash-phase short distances by slipping partially into its realm.

### Behavior & Ecology

- **Solitary and cunning**, avoids large groups unless hunting.
- **Habitat:** Dark forests, abandoned ruins, or zones where the veil between the shadow realm and material plane is thin.
- **Legendary Status:** Thought to be a **guardian of dark secrets** or the embodiment of fear. Often described in myths as an omen of death or the rise of powerful dark magic users.

### Realm Concept

- The shadow realm is dark and empty like you're, floating and time is distorted to the point you can't tell how long you were in there even if it's for a second.
- The Monocruph can appear in both realms at once, meaning attacks can come from **unexpected angles**, making it extremely hard to predict.
- Weak or untrained mages may get trapped in the shadow realm if attacked or caught off-guard.
