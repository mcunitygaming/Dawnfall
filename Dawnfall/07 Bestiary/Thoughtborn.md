The Thoughtborn are the "Demons" of Dawnfall
### **The Leading Thought**

**The Leading Thought** is the collective human desire for connection, possession, and control. It completely dominates the magic of **control**, allowing it to manipulate minds, raise the dead, possess people, and even partially influence the fundamental laws of **space, time, and matter** by forcing connections between them. It is the mastermind of the three and uses possessed humans as gateways from the Thoughtborn Realm into the Dawnfall Realm.

### **The Knowing Thought**

**The Knowing Thought** is the collective human desire for knowledge and understanding. It completely dominates the magic of **knowledge**, allowing it to know everything that has happened and perceive countless possible futures. It also has complete mastery over **all elemental magic**, including Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and every sub-element, making it the greatest elemental being in existence.

### **The Apex Thought**

**The Apex Thought** is the collective human desire for power and supremacy. It completely dominates the magic of **power**, giving it the greatest physical strength of any non-divine being. It can summon countless weapons, manipulate their weight and momentum, and wield every weapon as if it had mastered it for eternity. In raw physical combat, no mortal or Thought can surpass it.
