**Dragons of Aurvara**
## Overview

- Ancient, mythical species of beast.
- Masters of both **Physical Magic** and their innate **Elemental Magic**.
- Capable of catastrophic destruction even as young adults.
- Some dragons can reach **God-level power**, but most avoid interfering with lower beings.

### Physical Traits

- **Form:** Huge, bipedal, two-winged lizards.
- **Size:** Varies by age, but young adults can already level medium-sized towns.
- **Wings:** For flight, strengthened by Physical Magic.
- **Elemental Magic Pouch:** Specialized throat structure storing elemental energy for breath attacks.

### Abilities

1. **Elemental Breath Attack:**
    1. Releases the elemental energy stored in their throat pouch.
    2. Tied to their innate **Elemental Magic core**.
    3. Breath can range from **fire, water, air, or earth**, depending on the dragon’s type.

2. **Flight:**
    1. Strengthens the body with Physical Magic for lift and durability.

3. **Innate Magic Mastery:**
    1. Dragons are **born with fully formed elemental magic cores**, meaning they do not need to absorb particles to awaken elemental powers.
    2. Young dragons can already wield devastating elemental and physical magic in combat.

4. **God-Level Potential:**
    1. Some dragons reach **Ascendant-level strength**, rivaling gods in power.
    2. These dragons typically **avoid lower beings** unless provoked or involved in divine conflicts.

### Role in the World

- **Guardians of Nature & Balance:** Some dragons act as protectors of elemental sources, forests, mountains, and seas.
- **Beasts of Legend:** Many cultures in Aurvara **worship, fear, or hunt dragons**.
- **Elemental Alignment:** Each dragon type embodies a **specific elemental magic**, making them a perfect complement (or challenge) to your characters’ abilities.

### Interaction with Magic System

- Dragons are among the **only naturally born elemental magic core beings**, making them unique compared to other living things.
- Their **breath attacks** are literal, concentrated expressions of their elemental particles.
- They can also **use Physical Magic** to enhance offensive and defensive maneuvers.
- Because of their sheer magical power, dragons could play a role in **God Spirit awakenings** or **Ascendant-level trials**
