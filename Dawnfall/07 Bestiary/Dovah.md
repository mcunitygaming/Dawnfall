**or Dragons of Aurvara**
## Overview

- Ancient, mythical species of beast.
- Masters of their innate **Magic**.
- Capable of catastrophic destruction even as young adults.
- Some dragons can reach **God-level power**, but most avoid interfering with lower beings.
- Dragons are intelligent creatures just not to the level of the whole race of humans

### Physical Traits

- **Form:** Huge, bipedal, two-winged lizards.
- **Size:** Varies by age, but young adults can already level medium-sized towns.
- **Wings:** For flight, strengthened by Physical Magic.
- **Magic Pouch:** Specialized throat structure storing energy for breath attacks.

### Abilities

1. **Breath Attack:**
    1. Releases the energy stored in their throat pouch.
    2. Tied to their innate Magic cores.
    3. Breath can range from **any magic type**, depending on the dragon’s type.

2. **Flight:**
    1. Strengthens the body with Physical Magic for lift and durability.
    2. A dragon can use their magic for more than just breath they can do things light taking gas magic and using it to have more flight control

3. **Innate Magic Mastery:**
    1. Dragons are **born with fully formed a magic core**, meaning they do not need to absorb particles to awaken powers.
    2. Even Young dragons can already wield devastating magic in combat.

4. **God-Level Potential:**
    1. Some dragons reach **Ascendant-level strength**, rivaling gods in power.
    2. These dragons typically **avoid lower beings** unless provoked or involved in divine conflicts.

### Role in the World

- **Guardians of Nature & Balance:** Some dragons act as protectors of magic sources, forests, mountains, and seas.
- **Beasts of Legend:** Many cultures in Aurvara **worship, fear, or hunt dragons**.
- **Magic Alignment:** Each dragon type embodies a **specific magic type**, making them a perfect complement (or challenge) to your characters’ abilities.

### Interaction with Magic System

- Dragons are among the **only naturally mastered magic core beings**, making them unique compared to other living things.
- Their **breath attacks** are literal, concentrated expressions of their magic particles.
- They can also **use Physical Magic** to enhance offensive and defensive maneuvers.
- Because of their sheer magical power, dragons could play a role in **God Spirit / Vessel Frame awakenings** or **Ascendant-level trials**.
