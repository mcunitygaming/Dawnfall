## TTRPG Rules
### 1. CORE SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Aurvara is a part narrative-driven fantasy TTRPG where players take the role of **Sunbreak Spire students**—individuals selected from across the world to train in magic, combat, and reality-altering disciplines inside a pocket-dimension academy.

The game emphasizes:

- Identity-driven magic
- Tactical combat with adaptive learning systems
- Political and institutional roleplay
- Long-term character evolution
- Mystery discovery and world escalation

### 2. CORE MECHANIC SYSTEM

#### 2.1 Dice System

All actions are resolved using **1d100 (percentile system)**.

- Roll ≤ stat = success
- Roll > stat = failure
- Rolling under half your stat = critical success
- Rolling 95–100 = critical failure

#### 2.2 Core Stats (Cognitive Profile System)

Each character has 3 core mental stats:

- **Spatial Awareness (SA)** – perception, battlefield reading
- **Memory (ME)** – learning speed, spell retention
- **Processing Speed (PS)** – reaction time, decision-making

##### Standard Scale:

- 100 = average Sunbreak student

##### Character Sheet Format:

Current:  
SA / ME / PS  
  
Max Potential:  
SA / ME / PS

##### Usage:

- SA → dodge, awareness checks
- ME → spell learning, lore recall.
- PS → initiative, reaction combat

#### 2.3 Learning Distribution (Passive Trait System)

Each character allocates **100% learning affinity**:

- Verbal (instruction/leadership)
- Visual (observation/mimicry)
- Tactical (experience/instinct)

Example:

- Verbal 40%
- Visual 35%
- Tactical 25%

##### Mechanical Effect:

- Highest % grants +10 bonus to related checks
- Lowest % grants -5 penalty in opposite learning situations.

### 3. RACES OF AURVARA (PLAYABLE CLASSES)

Each race grants passive abilities.

#### Humans (Highborn Kin)

- Affinity: Fire Magic
- Trait: Adaptive Growth (+bonus XP gain when failing)
- Passive: Rapid Skill Diversification

#### Siravell

- Affinity: Water Magic
- Trait: Dual Form (land/water transformation)
- Passive: Ocean Resonance (immune to water pressure effects)

#### Sylfen

- Affinity: Air Magic
- Trait: Heightened Perception
- Passive: Slow Aging Memory Retention (no memory loss over time)

#### Deepstone Kin

- Affinity: Earth Magic
- Trait: Stonebody Resistance (+defense)
- Passive: Craft Mastery (creation magic bonuses)

#### Beastborn Kin

- Affinity: Physical Magic
- Trait: Instinct Drive
- Passive: Enhanced Physical Scaling (combat growth multiplier)

Subraces give minor bonuses:

- Wolf → tracking
- Cat → agility
- Bird → aerial mobility
- Lizard → endurance
- Deer → perception

### 4. MAGIC SYSTEM

Magic is divided into 4 major systems:

#### 4.1 Physical Magic (Universal)

- Exists in all living beings
- Used for reinforcement, combat, and bonding

##### Mechanics:

- Must be channeled through the body or a contact object
- Cannot remain stable outside contact range

##### Abilities:

- Strengthen body
- Enhance weapons
- Beast taming via infusion

#### 4.2 Elemental Magic (Core-Based System)

Requires creation of an elemental core.

##### Elements:

- Fire / Water / Earth / Air

##### Spell System:

Players may use:

- **Contracted Magic (Spells)** → stable, learned abilities
- **Uncontracted Magic** → freeform control (advanced users only)

##### Spellcasting Rule:

Roll:

- ME + Magic Control vs Difficulty DC

##### Elemental Fusion:

Combine elements for subtypes:

- Fire + Air = Lightning
- Fire + Earth = Magma
- Water + Air = Ice
- Water + Earth = Plant

#### 4.3 Irregular Magic (Rare Born System)

Only specific individuals possess it:

##### Space Magic

- Teleportation/portals
- Requires a known location

Types:

- Imperfect → limited travel, element-bound
- Perfect → full portal control

##### Time Magic (EXTREME RISK)

- Future sight (limited uses)
- Temporal freeze (heavy penalty)

Side effects:

- Paralysis
- Blindness progression
- Permanent disablement risk

##### Matter/Creation Magic

- Item creation
- Enhancement
- Spell engraving

Includes:

- Magic Imbue (add properties)
- Magic Script (store spells in objects)

#### 4.4 Mythic Magic (World-Breaking Tier)

##### Light Magic

- Healing
- Purification
- Buffing

Stronger during daylight

##### Dark Magic

- Corruption
- Power amplification
- Curse creation

Side effect:

- Dreadmire contamination
- Identity instability

##### Devil Spirit Mechanic (Boss Transformation)

Triggered when:

- Dark Magic user loses identity.

Effects:

- The host becomes the NPC boss entity.
- Gains massive stats
- Cannot be controlled

##### God Spirit System (Ascended Bond)

Triggered via:

- emotional breakthrough
- magical harmony

Effects:

- Passive ability enhancement
- Unique transformation form
- Semi-autonomous support entit

##### Vessel Frame (Physical Ascension)

Triggered by:

- extreme survival pressure

Effects:

- +25% body scaling
- stat multipliers:
    - STR +50%
    - SPD +40%
    - END +60%

Risk:

- identity drift toward Vessel form

### 5. SUNBREAK SPIRE SYSTEM

#### 5.1 Academy Structure

Players begin as 1st-year students.

Progression:

- Year 1–2: Foundation
- Year 3–4: Specialization + Missions

#### 5.2 Daily Structure

- 3–4 classes per day
- Lab's weekly
- Optional training at night

#### 5.3 Year 1–2 Curriculum

Core Classes:

- Magic Theory
- Physical Conditioning
- Combat Fundamentals
- Creation Magic Basics
- History of Aurvara
- Biology of Species

#### 5.4 Year 3–4 Specialization Tracks

Choose 1 major + 1 minor:

- Combat Magic
- Support / Ranged
- Creation / Biology
- Strategic Research
- God Vessel Studies (restricted)

#### 5.5 Missions System

Players may be assigned missions:

- Containment (monsters, corruption)
- Recovery (artifacts)
- Political (negotiation)
- Exploration (unknown zones)

### 6. COMBAT SYSTEM

#### Turn Order:

1. Processing Speed check
2. Initiative roll (PS + d100)

#### Actions per turn:

- 1 Major Action
- 1 Movement
- 1 Minor Action

#### Damage System:

- Physical Magic = STR scaling
- Elemental Magic = INT scaling
- Irregular Magic = special rules

#### Status Effects:

- Burn (Fire)
- Freeze (Water/Air)
- Crushed (Earth)
- Silence (Air disruption)
- Corruption (Dark Magic)

### 7. RING SYSTEM (PLAYER UTILITY ITEMS)

All players start with a Sunbreak Ring.

Functions:

- Inventory storage
- Teleportation (limited)
- Communication
- Emergency recall

#### Council Upgrade:

- group teleportation
- event control

#### Disciplinary Upgrade:

- tracking
- suppression authority

### 8. FACTIONS (NPC SYSTEM)

#### Student Council

- governance
- missions
- political influence

#### Disciplinary Committee

- enforcement
- combat response
- containment

### 9. WORLD STRUCTURE

#### Known Regions:

- Dawnlands (Humans)
- Northern Seas (Siravell)
- Western Forests (Sylfen)
- Mountain Cores (Deepstone Kin)
- Jungle Wilds (Beastborn)
- Unknown Deep Zones (Dreadmire regions)

### 10. GM TOOLKIT

#### Threat Scaling:

Enemies scale using tiers:

- Tier 1: Student level
- Tier 2: Elite student
- Tier 3: Faculty-level
- Tier 4: World threat
- Tier 5: Mythic entity

#### Dreadmire Corruption System:

Exposure causes:

- stat degradation
- hallucinations
- magic instability

Long-term:

- Devil Spirit transformation

#### Story Progression Arc:

1. Acceptance Test Arc
2. Academy Integration Arc
3. Rising Conflict Arc
4. Mission World Arc
5. Dawnfall Revelation Arc

### 11. WIN CONDITIONS

The campaign is not strictly win/lose.

Possible endings:

- Graduate Sunbreak
- Become a Council / Committee member.
- Awaken God Spirit
- Become Devil Spirit, boss.
- Discover the truth of Dawnfall.
- Survive the collapse of the magic system.

### FINAL NOTE (GM GUIDANCE)

Aurvara is designed as a **slow-burning mythic academy campaign** where:

- early game = training + identity building
- mid game = missions + political tension
- late game = reality-breaking magic escalation

The core theme of the system is:

Power is not what you can do — it is what you become.
